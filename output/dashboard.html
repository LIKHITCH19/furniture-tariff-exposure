Notes on the data

  • Effective rate = calculated duty / customs value of goods entered for consumption. It is an average across everything shipped under a product code, so a single item can sit above or below it.
  • November 2025 shows a one-month drop in duty on upholstered wood seating (940161) and kitchen cabinets (940340) from suppliers other than China, then a full recovery in December. I could not find a policy change that explains it, so it is left in the data and flagged here.
  • The BLS series covers "furniture, household goods", which is broader than HS chapter 94. It is the closest furniture import price series BLS still publishes monthly.

Sources: US Census Bureau international trade API · BLS Import/Export Price Indexes
